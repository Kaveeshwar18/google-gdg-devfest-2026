## Accepted proofs of present address

Passport Seva accepts any one of these as proof of present address:

1. Water bill
2. Telephone bill (landline or postpaid mobile)
3. Electricity bill
4. Income Tax assessment order
5. Election Commission photo ID card (voter ID)
6. Proof of gas connection
7. A certificate from the employer, if the employer is a reputed company, on its letterhead
8. A copy of the spouse's passport (first and last pages, including the family details page that names the applicant as spouse), but only if the applicant's present address matches the address in the spouse's passport
9. For minors, a copy of a parent's passport (first and last pages)
10. Aadhaar card
11. Rent agreement
12. A photo passbook of a running bank account

## Conditions on specific documents

- **Employer certificate:** only public limited companies can issue it, on company letterhead and with the company seal. Computerised printouts are not accepted.
- **Bank passbook:** only from scheduled public sector banks, scheduled private sector Indian banks and regional rural banks. The portal links a circular listing the accepted banks.
- **Minors:** a minor can submit present address proof that is in a parent's name.
- **Other Tatkaal documents:** a document from the Tatkaal list that shows the same present address as the application form may also be accepted as proof of residence.

## Aadhaar as address proof

The Aadhaar letter or card, or e-Aadhaar downloaded from the UIDAI website, is accepted as both proof of address and proof of photo identity for passport services. Acceptance depends on successful validation against the Aadhaar database. The portal says furnishing Aadhaar speeds up processing of the application.

## Rent agreements: registered or unregistered

MEA Office Memorandum No. VI/401/1/2/2015, dated 20 September 2017, deals with tenants. Earlier circulars from 2014 and 2015 had allowed a registered rent agreement as proof of address. Because many applicants hold only unregistered agreements, the 2017 memorandum directed passport offices to also accept an unregistered rent agreement as valid proof of address. The memorandum is linked from the address-proof list: https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/pdf/CIR_0169918_Y.pdf

## Only the present address needs proof

Applicants need proof only of their present address, however long they have lived there. The application form must still list every place the applicant stayed during the year before applying. Police use those addresses during verification.
