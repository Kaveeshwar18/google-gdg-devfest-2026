## Extra documents for Tatkaal

Tatkaal applicants submit the usual proofs of address, date of birth and non-ECR status, plus extra documents. Adults (over 18) add any three documents from the list below. Minors add any two from items 1 to 6.

1. Aadhaar: PVC card, the complete original letter, or e-Aadhaar with the UIDAI digital signature verified. Small cut-out or smart cards printed by anyone other than UIDAI are not accepted.
2. PAN card
3. Student photo ID card from a recognised educational institution
4. Birth certificate issued under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969
5. Ration card (latest or updated)
6. The last passport issued (re-issue only)
7. Voter ID (EPIC)
8. Driving licence (valid, and issued in the state where the application is made)
9. Bank, Kisan or Post Office passbook with the applicant's photo attested and a recent transaction
10. SC, ST or OBC certificate
11. Arms licence
12. Pension documents such as an ex-servicemen's pension book, a pension payment order, or a widow, dependant or old age pension certificate
13. Service photo ID from a state or central government body, PSU, local body or public limited company

Applicants also fill in and sign the "Undertaking for Tatkaal Scheme" (Hindi or English) and bring it to the appointment.

## Who cannot use Tatkaal

The scheme is not available for:

- any change in name
- a present address outside the jurisdiction of the RPO
- a minor's application with Annexure C (for example, parents divorced or separated)
- people repatriated at government cost, deported, or travelling on an Emergency Certificate
- people of Naga origin or of J&K origin, including minors
- applicants under sections 6(2)(e) or 6(2)(f) of the Passports Act (criminal court cases)
- applicants with an adverse police report, on a watch list, who have lost passports habitually, or who used forged passports

Fresh applications not eligible:
- citizens by registration or naturalisation
- adopted children
- a child born outside marriage with a single parent
- children born through surrogacy
- minors with a single parent

Re-issue applications not eligible:
- lost or stolen passports
- renewal of a short-validity passport
- passports damaged beyond recognition
- a change in sex, appearance or signature
- a change or correction of date or place of birth
- a change of father's, mother's or spouse's name

## Police verification and rescheduling under Tatkaal

The police verification FAQ says Tatkaal passports are not verified before issue. Verification is done after the passport is issued, as applicable. Since 1 April 2023, a Tatkaal appointment can be rescheduled only once after booking. Tatkaal appointments open at set times for each passport office, and the portal links the schedule. The fee is paid online at the full Tatkaal rate when booking. The passport office has the final say on issuing a passport under Tatkaal.
