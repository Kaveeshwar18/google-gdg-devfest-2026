## The core document set

For a fresh ordinary passport, the Passport Seva document advisory marks three items as mandatory:

1. proof of present address
2. proof of date of birth
3. proof of a non-ECR category, for applicants eligible for non-ECR status

Applicants bring the original of each document plus one set of self-attested photocopies to the Passport Seva Kendra. Each passport office's own home page may list extra documents, so applicants should check the page for their office. The advisory calls its lists indicative and says the Assistant or Regional Passport Officer's decision is final. Special situations add documents of their own, such as marriage, name changes, government employment, students living away from their parents, and residents of J&K or Nagaland. The advisory lets applicants select their case to see the full list.

## ECR and non-ECR status

ECR means Emigration Check Required. ECR status is printed in the passport of anyone who falls in the ECR category. Non-ECR passports carry no special mention, because the old non-ECR (ECNR) stamp has been discontinued. An ECR passport holder who goes abroad for certain work needs emigration clearance from the Protector General of Emigrants. Minors are non-ECR until they turn 18. When a minor's passport is reissued after they turn 18, they must show proof of a non-ECR category, or the passport will be marked ECR.

## Who qualifies for non-ECR and the proof needed

Common non-ECR categories and their proof:

- **Matriculation or higher education:** the pass certificate.
- **People over 50:** a birth certificate or school leaving certificate.
- **Income tax payers, their spouses and dependent children:** proof of tax assessment and payment for the last year, or a stamped return plus PAN. Advance tax alone or a nil return does not count.
- **Professional degree holders** (for example doctors, engineers, chartered accountants, teachers, advocates and accredited journalists): the degree certificate. Their spouse and children can also qualify, with marriage or birth proof.
- **Diploma holders** (NCVT or SCVT, or a three-year polytechnic diploma), nurses, and seamen with a Continuous Discharge Certificate.
- **People who have lived abroad for more than three years in total, and their spouses:** passport pages and an exit and entry statement.
- **Holders of permanent immigration visas.**
- **Gazetted government servants, their spouses and children:** Annexure A, G or H, or a relationship proof.

## Before and at the appointment

The applicant fills in the online form and receives an Application Reference Number (ARN). They must pay the fee online before an appointment can be booked. If the applicant does not visit the Kendra within 90 days of submitting the online form, the form has to be submitted again. Every applicant must attend the Passport Seva Kendra in person, and no agent or employee can submit for them. Children under 4 should bring a recent 4.5 x 3.5 cm photo with a white background.
