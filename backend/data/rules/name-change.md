## Two kinds of name change

The Consulate General of India in Jeddah publishes the name-change procedure for passport applicants it serves. It sorts name changes into two categories:

- **Category 1:** a woman changing her name after marriage, a divorcee or widow returning to her maiden name, and a remarried applicant changing name.
- **Category 2:** every other change of name.

Both categories need the application form, the original passport with copies of its key pages, photographs, the fee, and the applicant in person. Within India, the marriage-related changes are covered by Passport Seva's own document advisory (see the rule on name and spouse details after marriage).

## Extra documents for any other change of name

A Category 2 change, such as a new given name or a different surname not linked to marriage, needs two kinds of extra proof:

1. **Public notice of the change.** Either a notification in the Union or a State Official Gazette, or original cuttings of a name-change notice from two leading daily newspapers. One paper must circulate where the applicant now lives and the other where they permanently live.
2. **Use of the new name.** At least two public documents issued in the new name, to show the applicant has actually started using it.

## Tatkaal and name changes

This mission page says the Tatkaal service is not available for name-change applications. Other sources describe Tatkaal as allowed for minor spelling corrections or marriage-related changes, so the sources disagree. Before booking a Tatkaal appointment with a changed name, check the "Categories not under Tatkaal Scheme" list on passportindia.gov.in.

## Where this rule comes from

This procedure comes from an Indian mission page for applicants abroad (page last updated 15 October 2024). Passport Seva Kendras in India follow the same Passports Act and Rules, but local practice and extra document demands can differ. The Regional Passport Office makes the final decision. Passport Seva's police verification FAQ also tells applicants who changed their name in the past to attach the required name-change documents with the application.
