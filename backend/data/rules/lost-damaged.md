## Damaged versus damaged beyond recognition

Passport Seva sorts damaged passports into two kinds:

- **Damaged:** the passport number is readable, the name is legible and the photo is intact.
- **Damaged beyond recognition:** any of those is no longer true.

The difference matters for Tatkaal. A passport that is damaged but still legible can be reissued under Tatkaal, with the passport office having the final say. A passport damaged beyond recognition cannot.

## No duplicate passports

Passport Seva does not issue duplicate passports. After a loss or damage, the applicant applies under the re-issue category with the reason given as lost or damaged. They receive a new passport with a different number and fresh validity. A loss should be reported to the police straight away. If it happens abroad, it should also be reported to the nearest Indian mission or passport office.

## Documents for a lost or damaged passport

The re-issue application for a lost or damaged passport needs:

- proof of present address
- proof of date of birth
- an affidavit saying how and where the passport was lost or damaged (Annexure F)
- the police report in original
- self-attested copies of the old passport's first two and last two pages, including the ECR/non-ECR page, if available
- the original Emergency Certificate or seizure memo, if the applicant returned on one

Some cases also need confirmation from the Indian mission concerned. A photocopy of the lost passport is not compulsory, but the application form asks for its number, issue and expiry dates and place of issue. An applicant who does not know these should contact the passport office or mission.

## Loss abroad and urgent travel

A passport lost abroad should be reported at once to the local police and the Indian mission. To return to India urgently, the mission generally issues an Emergency Certificate after verifying the person's nationality and details. Back in India, the person applies for re-issue with the documents above. Tatkaal is not available for lost or stolen passports. It is also not available for anyone who travelled on an Emergency Certificate, and habitual losers of passports are excluded too.
