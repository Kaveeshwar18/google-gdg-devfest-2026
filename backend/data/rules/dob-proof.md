## Applicants born on or after 1 October 2023

For anyone born on or after 1 October 2023, Passport Seva accepts only one document as proof of date of birth: a birth certificate issued by the Registrar of Births and Deaths, a Municipal Corporation, or another authority empowered under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969. The portal lists no alternative documents for this group, so a school record, Aadhaar or an affidavit is not a substitute. Parents applying for a young child should get the birth registered and the certificate issued before booking.

## Accepted proofs for applicants born before 1 October 2023

Applicants born before 1 October 2023 can attach any one of these:

1. A birth certificate from the Registrar of Births and Deaths, a Municipal Corporation, or another authority empowered under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act, 1969.
2. A transfer, school leaving or matriculation certificate from the recognised school last attended or a recognised educational board, showing the date of birth.
3. A PAN card issued by the Income Tax Department, showing the date of birth.
4. For government servants, an extract of the service record; for retired government servants, the Pay Pension Order. Either must be attested or certified by the officer in charge of administration in the applicant's ministry or department and must show the date of birth.
5. A driving licence issued by the state Transport Department, showing the date of birth.
6. An Election Photo Identity Card (voter ID) from the Election Commission of India, showing the date of birth.
7. A policy bond from the Life Insurance Corporation of India or a public company, showing the policyholder's date of birth.

## The date must be exact

The portal adds a condition for items 3 to 6 (PAN card, service record or pension order, driving licence and voter ID). They count as proof of date of birth only if they show the precise date. A card that shows only a year of birth or an age does not qualify.

Aadhaar is not among the listed date-of-birth documents. The portal accepts Aadhaar as proof of address and photo identity, but an applicant relying on Aadhaar for date of birth should also carry one of the documents listed above.

## When documents show different dates

The date-of-birth list does not say which document prevails when two documents show different dates. The document advisory states that the listed requirements are indicative and that the final decision rests with the Assistant or Regional Passport Officer. For people who already hold a passport and need its date of birth changed or corrected, the advisory points to a separate circular rather than the general list. A date that differs between documents commonly causes problems at the counter or during police verification, so applicants should settle which date is correct before booking.
