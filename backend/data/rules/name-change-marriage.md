## Adding a spouse's name

To add a spouse's name to a passport, the document advisory accepts either:

- a marriage certificate, or
- a Joint Photo Declaration signed by both husband and wife (Annexure J).

Annexure J can be downloaded from the Passport Seva portal: https://www.passportindia.gov.in/AppOnlineProject/pdf/AnnexureJ.pdf

## Changing surname after marriage or divorce

A woman changing her surname after marriage needs either:

- a marriage certificate, or
- the Joint Photo Declaration (Annexure J).

If the change follows a divorce, she also needs the divorce order or decree. The advisory lists this surname change separately from a general change of name.

## Deleting or changing a spouse's name

- **To delete a spouse's name:** the divorce order or decree.
- **To replace a spouse's name with a new one:** the divorce order or decree, or the death certificate of the first spouse. In either case, also the remarriage certificate or the Joint Photo Declaration (Annexure J).

## Full change of name after marriage

The marriage documents above cover only a surname change or a spouse's name. If the applicant changes their full name, including the given name, the advisory says the standard name change procedure applies. That procedure needs extra proof of the new name (see the rule on general change of name). The listed documents are indicative. The final decision rests with the Assistant or Regional Passport Officer, and each passport office's home page may list extra local requirements.
