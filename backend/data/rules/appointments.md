## Paying and booking

Online payment is mandatory before booking an appointment at any Passport Seva Kendra or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra. Payment can be made by credit or debit card (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), internet banking, UPI or SBI wallet, through the SBI payment gateway. Tatkaal applicants pay the full Tatkaal fee online. A paid fee cannot be moved from one Application Reference Number (ARN) to another. The fee is valid for one year from the payment date, or one year from the first appointment date once an appointment is booked. The appointment SMS is accepted as proof of appointment, so a printed receipt is no longer required.

## How many times an appointment can be rescheduled

The portal gives two different limits for normal appointments. The fee payment FAQ says a confirmed appointment can be rescheduled only twice within a year of the first appointment date. The "process to apply" page says users can reschedule three times. Both agree that a Tatkaal appointment can be rescheduled only once (since 1 April 2023), and that a Police Clearance Certificate gets a single appointment within the year. Applicants who expect to need more than one change should check the limit shown on their own appointment screen.

## When money is deducted but payment shows pending or failed

- **Status "Pending":** check again the next working day using "Track Payment Status", then book with "Schedule Appointment" once the payment is confirmed.
- **Status "Failed" but the account was debited:** SBI refunds the amount to the applicant's bank within seven working days. After that, the applicant follows up with their own bank. If it is still not refunded, the applicant can email the SBI helpdesk listed on the FAQ with the ARN, transaction ID, amount and date.
- **Paid more than once:** the concerned Regional Passport Office refunds the extra payment.
- **Paid, but no appointment booked yet:** use "Schedule Appointment".

## Other appointment points

Passport Seva Kendras work Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, with tokens issued until 4:00 PM. Card payments carry a bank charge. The portal shows all charges on the payment screen.
